ASU-IGC-001 · Research context

Sources and related research

A route from the theory record to the established fields, evidence records and competing explanations that must be reviewed before stronger conclusions can be supported.

Evidence boundary: listing a research domain does not establish the ASU theory. Sources must be assessed for relevance, quality, limitations and competing interpretations.
01

Research domains

Fields relevant to critical evaluation

Established research domain

Developmental psychology

Individual development, learning, attachment, socialisation, adversity and variation across the life course.

Established research domain

Social and cultural learning

Transmission of behaviour, norms, knowledge and expectations through families, peers, institutions and media.

Competing explanations required

Cultural evolution and sociology

Institutional persistence, inequality, social networks, cultural change and historical context may explain patterns without a unified family “goal”.

High evidential threshold

Behavioural genetics and evolution

Genetic, environmental and interaction effects require careful separation. Short-term cultural change must not be described as biological evolution.

Where detailed source review belongs
